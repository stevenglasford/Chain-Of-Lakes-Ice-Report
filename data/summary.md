# Minneapolis Frozen Lakes Report: data summary

Ice thickness measured by hand through drilled holes on the lakes, ponds, channels and lagoons of Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA, about weekly while the ice lasts. 1252 observations (1220 with a measured thickness) from 2024-12-26 to 2026-03-11, over 2 winters.

Public domain (CC0 1.0). These are spot measurements at single drilled holes. Ice thickness varies a great deal over short distances and changes from day to day; a measurement here does not mean the ice is safe anywhere, including at the same spot later. Nothing in this dataset is safety advice.
